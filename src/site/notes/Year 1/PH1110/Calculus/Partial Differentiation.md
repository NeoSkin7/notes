A partial derivative is found as the derivative of a multivariate function with respect to a single variable, keeping all other variables constant:
$$
\left(\frac{\partial f(x,y)}{\partial x}\right)_y=\lim_{\delta x\to0}\left[\frac{f(x+\delta x,y)-f(x,y)}{\delta x}\right]
$$
where the multivariate function $f(x,y)=f_{xy}$ with variables $x$ and $y$ is being partially differentiated with respect to $x$, holding $y$ constant.
The partial derivative finds the gradient of the surface $f(x,y)$ at a point.
