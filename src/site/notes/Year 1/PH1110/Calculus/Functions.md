A function relates a domain (set) to those of a codomain (another set). The elements of the codomain which are related are called the range.
A function…
- Must work for all arguments (inputs)
- Give only one output value per argument

# Parity
Functions can be odd, even, or neither based on their symmetry about the $y$-axis ($x=0$).
````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
Even functions are symmetric:
$$
f(-x)=f(x)
$$
e.g. $y=\cos{x}$ or $y=x^2$.
```
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
Odd functions are asymmetric (reflected):
$$
f(-x)=-f(x)
$$
e.g. $y=\sin{x}$ or $y=x$.
```
````
---
The sum of two odd or even functions will always result in a function of the same symmetry:
$$
f_{\mathrm{odd}}(x) + g_{\mathrm{odd}}(x) = h_{\mathrm{odd}}(x)\\
f_{\mathrm{even}}(x) + g_{\mathrm{even}}(x) = h_{\mathrm{even}}(x)
$$
The product of two odd functions is even:
$$
f_{\mathrm{odd}}(x)\times g_{\mathrm{odd}}(x) = h_{\mathrm{even}}(x)
$$
However, the product of two even functions is also even:
$$
f_{\mathrm{even}}(x)\times g_{\mathrm{even}}(x) = h_{\mathrm{even}}(x)
$$
The product of an even and an odd function is odd:
$$
f_{\mathrm{even}}(x)\times g_{\mathrm{odd}}(x) = h_{\mathrm{odd}}(x)
$$
But, the sum of an even and an odd function is neither odd nor even (no symmetry).
